Discussion Text

What Is a Discussion Text?

A discussion text is a text that presents different points of view about an issue. It usually discusses arguments for and against a particular topic.

Social Function

The social function of a discussion text is to present different opinions about an issue before reaching a conclusion.

Generic Structure

1. Issue

Introduces the topic or problem being discussed.

2. Arguments For

Presents supporting opinions or positive arguments.

3. Arguments Against

Presents opposing opinions or negative arguments.

4. Conclusion

Provides a final statement based on the discussion.

Language Features

  • Simple Present Tense
  • Connectives: however, furthermore, therefore
  • Modal verbs: should, can, must
  • Expressions of opinion

Example: Travelling by Plane or Train?

Discussion Topic

Travelling is one of the most enjoyable activities because it allows people to explore new places, meet different people, and experience new cultures. When planning a trip, choosing the right transportation is an important decision. Some people prefer travelling by plane because it saves time, especially when visiting places far away. Others prefer travelling by train because it offers a more relaxing journey and beautiful views along the way. However, both transportation options have their own advantages and disadvantages. Travellers often consider several things, such as ticket prices, travel time, comfort, and distance, before making a decision. So, between travelling by plane and train, which one is the better choice?

Issue

Travelling by plane and train is a popular way to visit different places. However, both means of transportation have different advantages and disadvantages.

Arguments For

Travelling by plane is faster and suitable for long-distance journeys. Meanwhile, travelling by train offers comfortable seats and beautiful scenery along the way.

Arguments Against

Plane tickets are often more expensive, especially during holiday seasons. On the other hand, trains usually take more time to reach distant destinations.

Conclusion

Both planes and trains have advantages and disadvantages. Therefore, travellers should choose the transportation that best suits their needs, budget, and travel plans.

Simple Present Tense

What Is Simple Present Tense?

Simple Present Tense is used to express daily activities, habits, general facts, and routines. It helps us talk about things that happen regularly or are generally true.

Functions

01. Daily Routines

To describe activities that we do every day.

02. Habits

To express activities that we do regularly.

03. General Facts

To state facts that are generally true.

04. Regular Activities

To describe activities that happen repeatedly.

Sentence Patterns

POSITIVE (+)

Positive Sentence

Subject + V1(s/es) + Object

Example:

She visits Bali every year.

NEGATIVE (-)

Negative Sentence

Subject + do/does + not + V1

Example:

She does not visit Bali every year.

INTERROGATIVE (?)

Interrogative Sentence

Do/Does + Subject + V1 + Object?

Example:

Does she visit Bali every year?

Do and Does

DO

Used with:

I   You   We   They

Example: Do they like travelling?

DOES

Used with:

He   She   It

Example: Does she like travelling?

Remember!

In negative and interrogative sentences, always use the base form of the verb (V1). Do not add -s or -es after the verb when using do or does.

Examples

01

I travel with my family.

02

She visits the beach every weekend.

03

They do not travel by plane.

04

Does he like travelling?

Time Expressions

What Are Time Expressions?

Time expressions are words or phrases that tell us when an activity happens. They help us understand the time, frequency, or period of an activity. In English, time expressions are often used with the Simple Present Tense to describe daily routines and habits.

Common Time Expressions

📅

Every Day

Used for activities that happen each day.

Example

I study English every day.

🗓️

Every Weekend

Used for activities that happen every weekend.

Example

We visit our grandparents every weekend.

🔄

Usually

Used for activities that happen most of the time.

Example

She usually travels by bus.

⏳

Sometimes

Used for activities that happen occasionally.

Example

They sometimes go to the beach.

⭐

Always

Used for activities that happen all the time.

Example

He always brings a camera when travelling.

Other Examples

In the morning
At night
On Monday
Once a week
Twice a month

Common Mistakes

Let's Learn from Mistakes!

Common mistakes are errors that often happen when students use English. Understanding these mistakes can help us improve our grammar and use English more correctly. Let's learn how to identify and correct them!

Common Grammar Mistakes

01

Mistake 1

✕ Incorrect

She go to Bali every year.

✓ Correct

She goes to Bali every year.

Why?

Use -s or -es for verbs with He, She, and It in positive Simple Present sentences.

02

Mistake 2

✕ Incorrect

They goes to the beach.

✓ Correct

They go to the beach.

Why?

Use the base form of the verb with They. Do not add -s or -es to the verb.

03

Mistake 3

✕ Incorrect

He don't like travelling.

✓ Correct

He doesn't like travelling.

Why?

Use doesn't with He, She, and It in negative Simple Present sentences.

04

Mistake 4

✕ Incorrect

Does she visits the museum?

✓ Correct

Does she visit the museum?

Why?

After does, use the base form of the verb (V1). Do not add -s or -es to the verb.

READING CHALLENGE

Reading Practice

A Wonderful Trip to Bali

Last holiday, my family and I visited Bali. Bali is one of the most beautiful islands in Indonesia. It has amazing beaches, traditional culture, and delicious food.

On the first day, we visited Kuta Beach. The beach had beautiful scenery and golden sand. Many tourists enjoyed swimming, playing, and taking pictures.

On the second day, we visited Tanah Lot Temple. The temple stands on a large rock near the sea. It is one of the most famous tourist attractions in Bali.

During the trip, we also tried traditional Balinese food. The food was delicious, and the people were friendly.

I learned that travelling is not only about visiting beautiful places but also about understanding different cultures. It was an unforgettable experience.

1. Where did the writer spend the holiday?

2. Which place did the writer visit on the first day?

3. What is Tanah Lot Temple famous for?

4. How did the writer describe the Balinese people?

5. Why do many tourists visit Kuta Beach?

6. What did the writer do during the trip besides visiting tourist places?

7. What does the word "unforgettable" in the last paragraph mean?

8. What is the main idea of the third paragraph?

9. Based on the text, what can students learn from the writer's travelling experience?

10. If you wanted to learn about Balinese culture, which activity would be most suitable based on the text?

11. Which statement best represents the writer's opinion about travelling?

12. Imagine you have limited time to visit Bali. Based on the text, which choice would give you both natural and cultural experiences?
